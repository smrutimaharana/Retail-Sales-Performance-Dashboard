# Retail-Sales-Performance-Dashboard

Project Overview

The Retail Sales and Performance Insights Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI. The main objective of this project is to analyze retail sales data, evaluate business performance, identify profitable product categories, and understand sales trends across different regions and cities.

This dashboard transforms raw retail data into meaningful visual insights that help businesses make better data-driven decisions and improve their overall performance.

Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify top-performing product categories.
- Understand the relationship between discounts and profitability.
- Track sales trends over different order dates.
- Compare sales performance across different cities and regions.
- Identify opportunities to increase revenue and improve profit margins.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Sales: 15 Million
- Total Profit: 3.75 Million
- Profitability Analysis: Evaluate overall business performance and profitability.

Dashboard Features

1. Sales Performance Analysis
Visualizes total sales across different product categories to identify major revenue contributors.

2. Category-Wise Sales Analysis
Compares sales performance across categories such as Eggs, Meat & Fish, Snacks, Food Grains, Bakery, Fruits & Veggies, Beverages, and Oil & Masala.

3. Discount and Profit Analysis
Uses scatter plot visualization to understand how different discount levels influence profit across product categories.

4. Time-Based Sales Analysis
Tracks sales performance over different order dates to identify fluctuations and changing sales patterns.

5. City-Wise Performance Analysis
Compares sales and profit across different cities to identify high-performing geographical markets.

6. Interactive Filters
Includes region and order-date filters to allow users to explore specific business segments and analyze data dynamically.

Key Insights

- Total sales reached 15M, while total profit accounted for 3.75M.
- Eggs, Meat & Fish, Snacks, and Food Grains are among the major sales-contributing categories.
- The discount analysis indicates that higher discounts do not always guarantee higher profits.
- Certain cities, including Kanyakumari, Vellore, and Tirunelveli, show strong sales performance.
- Sales patterns vary across different order dates, highlighting opportunities for further business analysis.

Business Recommendations

- Discount Optimization: Monitor discount levels and avoid excessive discounts that may reduce profit margins.
- Product Bundling: Combine fast-moving products with other product categories to increase average order value.
- Inventory Management: Maintain sufficient stock of high-demand products to minimize stockouts.
- Regional Growth: Focus on high-performing cities while identifying opportunities in lower-performing markets.
- Profit Improvement: Regularly evaluate category-wise sales, discounts, and profit to develop better pricing strategies.
